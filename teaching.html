Teaching

ΓΕ0104: Partial Differential Equations. Lecturer. Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Thessaly, Greece. The course in the forth semester of the undergraduate program. Curriculum includes: First order PDEs, Method of characteristics, Weak and strong solutions, Second order PDEs, Classification of second order PDEs, Wave equation in infinite and semi-infinite domains (D'Alembert's solution), Fourier series, Method of sepration of variables, Heat equation, Wave equation, Laplace's equation.
